## Writing a new post

Create a file in `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` with front matter like
the block at the top of this file, then write your content in Markdown below it.

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "My second post"
date: 2026-09-10 12:00:00 +0000
---

Post body goes here.
```

Commit and push to the `main` branch and GitHub Pages rebuilds the site
automatically within a minute or two.
